About the practice

Thirty years of family wellness on Guam

A clinic founded on a simple idea: Guam families deserve to feel well, move freely, and stay that way.

Drs. Rob and Barbara Gregory opened a small chiropractic center in Tamuning with a vision to improve quality of life across the island. The practice has grown the only way that lasts: referrals from patients who got better and told their family. Today the Gregorys and Dr. Megumi Homma care for multiple generations under one roof, including their own.

Pika’s Best of Guam 2022 winner for Best Chiropractor/Clinic.

RG

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Robert Gregory, D.C.

Originally from Galesburg, Michigan, Dr. Rob has made Guam home. He played Division I and semi-professional soccer and has coached for more than 25 years. Together with Dr. Barbara, he has volunteered countless hours to the soccer community and civic organizations.

BG

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Barbara Gregory, D.C.

Born in the Philippines and raised on Guam, daughter of John “Eddie” Onedera, a CHamoru native, and Olivia Rivera Onedera. After a teenage auto accident left her in debilitating pain, chiropractic gave her her life back. She co-founded this clinic to give that same hope to generations of Guam families.

MH

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Megumi Homma, D.C.

Originally from Los Angeles. UCLA undergraduate. Doctor of Chiropractic from Parker University. Webster Technique and Applied Kinesiology certified. Candidate for Diplomate in Clinical Chiropractic Pediatrics. The doctor parents book for babies, kids, and pregnancy.
